External widgets

Pages other people made that already live online, catalogued here with credit to their authors. Set them up, then download a small file for iiSU that shows the page at the right size. They need an internet connection.

Preview

How these work

  • Download gives a small .html file that loads the page and scales it to the widget, so a page made for a full screen still fits a 198 px tile.
  • Copy link gives the page's own address with your settings, for apps that take a URL.
  • Credits on each card links to the author and the source. Everything here belongs to its authors.
  • Know a page that would make a good widget? Add it to assets/external.js.
