Adding a widget to iiSU

  1. Open this page on your handheld and press Download (or download on a computer and copy the file over).
  2. In iiSU, add a web widget and pick the downloaded .html file.
  3. To change a setting later, come back here and download it again. Your choices on this page are remembered.

Good to know

  • Two sizes. Each widget has a compact tile and a larger layout when you focus it in iiSU. Use Preview above to see both.
  • Location. iiSU does not give widgets GPS access, so Automatic weather uses the approximate location of your internet connection (a VPN changes it). Pick a city for an exact place.
  • Tilt effect. Uses the handheld's motion sensor to move the content a little as you tilt it. The Scene widget always has it (unless you turn off its tilt motion).
  • Sound. The chime and the games make sound through the handheld's speaker. The chime stays silent while a game is running unless you change that.
  • Offline. Widgets keep their last data on the device and show it straight away, even after a restart or without a connection.
  • Hosted links. Copy link gives an address with the settings in it, for apps that load a widget from a URL instead of a file.
